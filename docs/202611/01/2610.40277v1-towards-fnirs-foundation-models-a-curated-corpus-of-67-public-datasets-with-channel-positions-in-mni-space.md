## 摘要
脑基础模型通过大规模预训练减少了对任务特定数据的依赖，从而改变了医学人工智能和计算神经科学。尽管这些模型在无创神经影像数据上已取得成功，但其在功能性近红外光谱（fNIRS）中的应用一直滞后，原因是公共数据集规模有限且异质性较高，这些数据是在不同的实验方案、设备和传感器配置下采集的。此外，没有任何一个fNIRS数据集在规模上能与功能性磁共振成像（fMRI）、脑电图（EEG）或脑磁图（MEG）中最大的数据集相比。fNIRS数据集还分散在不同平台上，并且往往信息不足，或缺乏使其更易于使用的实验方案。鉴于EEG基础模型的进展使得跨多样EEG导联组合的灵活建模成为可能，我们整理了来自67个公共数据集的1,955小时fNIRS数据，涵盖2,798名参与者，这是迄今规模最大的fNIRS数据整理工作。整理后的数据包括氧合血红蛋白（HbO）和脱氧血红蛋白（HbR）信号，以及每个通道在蒙特利尔神经学研究所（MNI）标准空间中的三维位置，从而使不同数据集之间的通道位置具有可比性。我们在 https://github.com/sposso/fnirs-datasets 发布了可自动处理每个数据集的数据加载器，以及用于准备数据的最小预处理流程。本工作旨在促进未来fNIRS基础模型的发展。

## Abstract
Brain foundation models have transformed medical artificial intelligence and computational neuroscience by reducing the reliance on task-specific data through large-scale pretraining. While they have been successful with non-invasive neuroimaging data, their adoption in functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) has lagged due to the limited size and heterogeneity of public datasets, which are collected under varying protocols, devices, and sensor configurations. In addition, no single fNIRS dataset is comparable in size to the largest datasets in functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI), electroencephalography (EEG), or magnetoencephalography (MEG). The fNIRS datasets are also spread across different platforms and often have insufficient information, or lack the protocols that would make them easier to use. Given the advances in EEG foundation models that enable flexible modeling across diverse EEG montages, we curated 1,955 hours of fNIRS data from 67 public datasets spanning 2,798 participants, representing the largest fNIRS curation effort to date. The curated data include oxygenated (HbO) and deoxygenated (HbR) hemoglobin signals along with the three-dimensional position of every channel in the standard space of the Montreal Neurological Institute (MNI), which makes positions comparable across datasets. We release dataloaders that handle each dataset automatically, along with the minimal preprocessing pipeline used to prepare the data, at https://github.com/sposso/fnirs-datasets. This work aims to facilitate the future development of foundation models for fNIRS.
