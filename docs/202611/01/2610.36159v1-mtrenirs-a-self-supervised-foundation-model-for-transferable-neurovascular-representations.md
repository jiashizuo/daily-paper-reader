## 摘要
功能性近红外光谱（fNIRS）为在自然场景中监测神经活动的血流动力学相关指标提供了一种有前景的方法。然而，由于采集设备和光极阵列配置的高度异质性，现有fNIRS建模方法大多局限于小规模、单次会话且采样率较低的数据。此外，fNIRS信号复杂的时空结构进一步限制了模型的可扩展性及其跨设置的可迁移性。因此，目前尚不清楚单一fNIRS编码器能否学习到可跨受试者、任务、设备和阵列配置使用的表征。在这里，我们表明，一个阵列感知的掩码自编码器在涵盖268名受试者和567记录小时的12个公开fNIRS数据集上预训练后，能够学习到可泛化的神经血管表征，并可迁移至三个外部数据集。我们的fNIRS基础模型MtReNIRS通过将异质阵列映射到共享解剖参考，联合编码fNIRS的慢速血流动力学特性、发色团身份和皮层位置。MtReNIRS在跨受试者性能上与全监督基线相当或更优，并在少样本受试者内校准中表现优异，优于先前最先进的fNIRS模型，同时对稀疏阵列保持稳健。我们的结果表明，阵列感知自监督能够产生具有生理基础、可迁移的fNIRS表征，为标签高效的脑机接口提供可扩展的基础。

## Abstract
Functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) offers a promising approach for monitoring the hemodynamic correlates of neural activity in naturalistic settings. However, existing modeling methods for fNIRS are largely confined to small-scale, single-session data with low sampling rates, driven by high heterogeneity in acquisition devices and optode montages. In addition, the complex spatiotemporal structure of fNIRS signals further restricts model scalability and transferability across settings. As a result, it remains unclear whether a single fNIRS encoder can learn representations that are usable across subjects, tasks, devices, and montages. Here, we show that a montage-aware masked autoencoder, pretrained on 12 public fNIRS datasets spanning 268 subjects and 567 recording hours, learns generalizable neurovascular representations that transfer across three external datasets. Our fNIRS foundation model, MtReNIRS, jointly encodes the slow hemodynamic nature of fNIRS, chromophore identity and cortical location by mapping heterogeneous montages onto a shared anatomical reference. MtReNIRS achieves cross-subject performance on par or better than fully supervised baselines and excels in few-shot within-subject calibration, where it outperforms prior state-of-the-art fNIRS models, while remaining robust to sparse montages. Our results indicate that montage-aware self-supervision yields physiologically grounded, transferable fNIRS representations, providing a scalable foundation for label-efficient brain-computer interfaces.
