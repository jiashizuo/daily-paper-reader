## 摘要
大分子池分数（MPF）是一种定量成像生物标志物，对髓鞘完整性及组织大分子含量的其他变化敏感，但专用采集和重建方法限制了其在大规模研究中的应用。我们提出一种物理引导的自监督深度学习框架，可直接从常规临床脑MRI（T1加权、T2加权和液体衰减反转恢复[FLAIR]）回顾性估计MPF，而无需配对的定量参考扫描。该框架嵌入了分析型双池磁化传递信号模型，该模型将序列平均的射频发射功率与表观纵向弛豫相关联。为适应异质性临床协议，网络通过自适应实例归一化使中间特征以采集参数为条件。该框架在一个多中心、多厂商、多协议的数据集上进行了训练和评估，该数据集包含7,705次MRI检查（3 T）。在1,237次测试检查中，白质平均MPF为0.182 ± 0.007，灰质为0.144 ± 0.009，不同厂商×协议组间变异有限（组间变异系数分别为2.65%和5.63%）。在70次带有脱髓鞘疾病标签的检查中，分割病灶内的MPF较表现正常白质低35.6%。在健康志愿者中采集的两种不同临床协议所得的MPF图显示，平均体素绝对百分比差异为7.7%，一致性相关系数为0.888，表明良好的跨协议一致性。这些结果表明，融合磁化传递信息的自监督学习能够从常规临床MRI实现稳定的MPF映射，为在既有影像档案和前瞻性采集的常规临床扫描中研究脱髓鞘病理提供了一种可扩展的方法，而无需专门的定量磁化传递（qMT）采集。

## Abstract
Macromolecular pool fraction (MPF) is a quantitative imaging biomarker sensitive to myelin integrity and other changes in tissue macromolecular content, but specialized acquisitions and reconstruction methods limit its use in large-scale studies. We present a physics-guided, self-supervised deep learning framework for retrospectively estimating MPF directly from conventional clinical brain MRI ($T_1$-weighted, $T_2$-weighted, and fluid-attenuated inversion recovery [FLAIR]) without paired quantitative reference scans. The framework embeds an analytical two-pool magnetization transfer signal model that related sequence-averaged radiofrequency transmit power to apparent longitudinal relaxation. To accommodate heterogeneous clinical protocols, the network conditions intermediate features on acquisition parameters via adaptive instance normalization. It was trained and evaluated on a multisite, multivendor, multiprotocol dataset of 7,705 MRI sessions (3 T). Across 1,237 test sessions, mean MPF was $0.182 \pm 0.007$ in white matter and $0.144 \pm 0.009$ in gray matter, with limited variation across vendor-by-protocol groups (between-group coefficient of variation: 2.65% and 5.63%, respectively). In 70 sessions with demyelinating-disease labels, MPF was 35.6% lower in segmented lesions than in normal-appearing white matter. MPF maps from two different clinical protocols acquired in a healthy volunteer showed a mean voxel-wise absolute percentage difference of 7.7% and a concordance correlation coefficient of $0.888$, indicating good cross-protocol consistency. These results suggest that magnetization transfer-informed self-supervised learning enables stable MPF mapping from conventional clinical MRI, providing a scalable approach to investigate demyelinating pathology in both existing imaging archives and prospectively acquired conventional clinical scans without dedicated qMT acquisitions.
