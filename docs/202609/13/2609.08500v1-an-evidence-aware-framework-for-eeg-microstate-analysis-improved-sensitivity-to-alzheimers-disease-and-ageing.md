## 摘要
脑电（EEG）微状态分析通常将每个头皮地形图转换为赢者通吃的硬标签，并使用持续时间、出现率、覆盖率、转移和符号复杂度来总结所得序列。尽管这种读出具有可解释性，但它丢弃了证据强度、分配模糊性和低置信度时段。我们引入一种模板证据轨迹框架，该框架在每个采样的全局场功率（GFP）峰值处保留所有模板或受试者特异性地形社区的证据。传统硬标签被视为这一多变量轨迹的压缩读出。我们推导出经典描述符的两种证据感知扩展：高证据片段持续时间和片段发生率，用于量化强状态证据的时间聚集与碎片化；以及空状态 Lempel-Ziv 复杂度（LZC），用于显式编码证据不足时段。我们在四个静息态EEG数据集上评估了该框架，这些数据集涵盖阿尔茨海默病和年龄相关变异。固定状态模型包括 K = 4 和 K = 7 的 K-means、AAHC 和 HMM，以及自适应的受试者特异性 Leiden 和 Infomap 社区。在 32 个数据集-模型案例中，轨迹衍生的持续时间效应在代表性设置下的所有案例中均超过匹配的硬标签效应，并在更广泛的参数网格中于 30-32 个案例中超过。空状态 LZC 在大多数情况下优于传统 LZC，而分类在轨迹或组合特征上表现出适度但一致的增益。因此，保留模板证据可提供对EEG地形状态动态更敏感的读出，同时保持与传统微状态分析兼容。

## Abstract
Electroencephalography (EEG) microstate analysis commonly converts each scalp topography into a winner-take-all hard label and summarises the resulting sequence using duration, occurrence, coverage, transitions, and symbolic complexity. Although interpretable, this readout discards evidence strength, assignment ambiguity, and low-confidence periods. We introduce a template evidence trajectory framework that retains, at each sampled Global Field Power (GFP) peak, the evidence for all templates or subject-specific topographic communities. Conventional hard labels are treated as a compressed readout of this multivariate trajectory. We derive two evidence-aware extensions of classical descriptors: high-evidence episode duration and episode rate, which quantify temporal clustering and fragmentation of strong state evidence, and null-state Lempel-Ziv complexity (LZC), which explicitly encodes insufficient-evidence periods. We evaluated the framework across four resting-state EEG datasets spanning Alzheimer's disease and age-related variation. Fixed-state models included K-means, AAHC, and HMMs at K = 4 and K = 7, together with adaptive subject-specific Leiden and Infomap communities. Across 32 dataset-model cases, trajectory-derived duration effects exceeded matched hard-label effects in all cases at the representative setting and in 30-32 cases across a broader parameter grid. Null-state LZC improved over traditional LZC in most cases, while classification showed modest but consistent gains for trajectory or combined features. Retaining template evidence therefore provides a more sensitive readout of EEG topographic state dynamics while remaining compatible with conventional microstate analysis.
