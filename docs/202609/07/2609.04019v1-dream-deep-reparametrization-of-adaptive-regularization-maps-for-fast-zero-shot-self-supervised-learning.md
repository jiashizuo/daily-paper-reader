## 摘要
自适应正则化是提高经典变分重建方法灵活性的有效手段，同时保留其可解释性和数学结构。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种通过算法展开在零样本自监督设置中学习针对全变分（TV）和全广义变分（TGV）的深度重参数化自适应正则化图（DREAM）的方法，无需访问大型配对数据集。我们首先在二维图像去噪问题上验证DREAM，然后将其应用于大规模动态MRI重建。所得到的自适应模型具有理论基础，因为它们保留了作为展开架构构建块的原-对偶算法的收敛性质。我们的结果表明，正则化参数图可以被高效学习，与最先进的方法相比，需要更少的权重更新即可实现相当的性能。我们进一步证明，这些图的CNN参数化充当隐式先验，提供有效的降维，优于自适应参数的直接优化。在这两个应用中，DREAM的监督训练和零样本训练之间的性能差距小于用于比较的端到端深度学习方法。值得注意的是，在动态心脏MRI重建中，零样本训练与监督模型的性能相当。此外，学习到的自适应图高度可解释，在难以获得参考数据时，为纯深度学习方法提供了一种有吸引力的替代方案。

## Abstract
Adaptive regularization is an effective means of improving the flexibility of classical variational reconstruction methods while retaining their interpretability and mathematical structure. In this work, we propose an approach for learning deep-reparameterized adaptive regularization maps (DREAM) for Total Variation (TV) and Total Generalized Variation (TGV) through algorithm unrolling in a zero-shot, self-supervised setting, requiring no access to large paired datasets. We first validate DREAM on a two-dimensional image-denoising problem and then apply it to large-scale dynamic MRI reconstruction. The resulting adaptive models are theoretically grounded, as they retain the convergence properties of the primal-dual algorithm used as the building block of the unrolled architecture. Our results show that the regularization parameter maps can be learned efficiently, requiring substantially fewer weight updates than state-of-the-art methods to achieve comparable performance. We further demonstrate that the CNN parametrization of the maps acts as an implicit prior and provides effective dimensionality reduction, outperforming direct optimization of the adaptive parameters. Across both applications, the performance gap between supervised and zero-shot training is smaller for DREAM than for the end-to-end deep-learning methods considered for comparison. Remarkably, in dynamic cardiac MRI reconstruction, zero-shot training matches the performance of the supervised model. Moreover, the learned adaptive maps are highly interpretable, providing a compelling alternative to purely deep-learning-based methods when reference data are difficult to obtain.
