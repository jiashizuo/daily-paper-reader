## 摘要
TopAneu 2026挑战要求在CT和MR血管造影中均对颅内动脉瘤进行体素级分割，并将其归入五十多个血管特异性类别之一。我们采用一个六阶段的解剖感知流程来解决该问题，而非单一分割网络。逻辑回归分类器将每个扫描路由至特定模态的分支；网络对血管系统进行分割，先得到二值掩膜，再在完整的36类动脉分类体系上进行分割；候选定位器将原始血管造影和血管先验转化为可疑区域；以候选区域为中心的网络在每个裁剪块中分割病灶；连通分量成为实例；并由逐实例分类器为每个实例分配其解剖位置。该设计遵循两项原则。每当上游模型输出成为下游训练输入时，该输出均以折外方式生成，从而没有任何阶段会基于自身过于干净的预测进行训练。并且解剖决策按病灶进行一次，而非按体素进行一次，这与数据相符：在完整发布版中的406个连通动脉瘤分量中，405个恰好带有一个位置标签。任务1从任务2的掩膜中读取，从而在构造上使二者保持一致。我们报告各阶段性能，并进行五部分消融，以分离血管先验和类别感知采样在每个阶段的贡献。

## Abstract
The TopAneu 2026 challenge requires intracranial aneurysms to be segmented at the voxel level and assigned to one of more than fifty vessel-specific classes, on CT and MR angiography alike. We address it with a six-stage anatomy-aware pipeline rather than a single segmentation network. A logistic-regression classifier routes each scan to a modality-specific branch; networks segment the vasculature, first as a binary mask and then over the full 36-class arterial taxonomy; a candidate localiser turns raw angiography and vessel priors into suspicious regions; a candidate-centred network segments the lesion in each crop; connected components become instances; and a per-instance classifier assigns each its anatomical location. Two principles organise the design. Whenever an upstream model output becomes a downstream training input it is generated out-of-fold, so no stage trains on unrealistically clean predictions of itself. And the anatomical decision is made once per lesion rather than once per voxel, which fits the data: of 406 connected aneurysm components in the full release, 405 carry exactly one location label. Task 1 is read off the Task 2 mask, making the two consistent by construction. We report stage-wise performance and a five-part ablation isolating what vessel priors and class-aware sampling contribute at each stage.
