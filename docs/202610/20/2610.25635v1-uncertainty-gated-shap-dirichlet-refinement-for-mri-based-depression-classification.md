## 摘要
由于解剖学改变细微且异质，基于结构 MRI 的重性抑郁障碍（MDD）分类仍具挑战性。我们提出不确定性门控的 SHAP-Dirichlet 细化方法，这是一种事后可靠性层，可在不重新训练分类器的情况下改进预测。该方法检索相似的带标签 SHAP 解释，构建 Dirichlet 类别证据，并通过概率融合选择性地细化不确定预测，同时允许将不可靠病例推迟以供专家复核。我们在 504 名 SRPBS 受试者（252 名对照和 252 名 MDD 患者）上，使用 159 个解剖学特征和重复嵌套交叉验证评估了五种分类器。细化在 TabM 和 ResNet 的所有 8 次重复中均提高了准确率，平均提升分别为 1.04 和 0.45 个百分点（符号检验 p=0.008），同时最多仅修改 8.3% 的预测。增益集中在低置信度病例中，并在校准方面有显著改善。该方法也使 RBF 支持向量机受益，但对逻辑回归或随机森林无益。消融实验证实了不确定性门控和软融合的重要性。Dirichlet 证据可识别基线错误（AUROC 0.62），而推迟 50% 的病例可使保留病例的准确率提高至约 75%。这些发现支持将选择性解释引导的细化作为一种实用方法，以提高预测可靠性并促进 MDD 分类中的专家分诊。

## Abstract
Structural MRI-based classification of major depressive disorder (MDD) remains challenging due to subtle, heterogeneous anatomical changes. We propose uncertainty-gated SHAP-Dirichlet refinement, a post-hoc reliability layer that improves predictions without retraining the classifier. The method retrieves similar labeled SHAP explanations, constructs Dirichlet class evidence, and selectively refines uncertain predictions through probabilistic fusion, while allowing unreliable cases to be deferred for expert review. We evaluate five classifiers on 504 SRPBS subjects (252 controls and 252 MDD patients) using 159 anatomical features and repeated nested cross-validation. Refinement improved accuracy across all 8 repetitions for TabM and ResNet, yielding mean gains of 1.04 and 0.45 percentage points (sign test p=0.008), while modifying at most 8.3% of predictions. Gains were concentrated among low-confidence cases, with significant improvements in calibration. The method also benefited an RBF support vector machine but not logistic regression or random forests. Ablations confirmed the importance of uncertainty gating and soft fusion. Dirichlet evidence identified baseline errors (AUROC 0.62), while deferring 50% of cases increased retained-case accuracy to approximately 75%. These findings support selective explanation-guided refinement as a practical approach to improving prediction reliability and facilitating expert triage in MDD classification.
