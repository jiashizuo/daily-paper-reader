## 摘要
引言：扩散MRI可通过将微米级扩散聚合到毫米尺度，在活体内探测脑组织架构，但交叉的白质纤维会降低下游任务（如微结构建模和纤维束成像）的敏感性。当前基于fixel的方法用于识别纤维的数量、方向和散布，依赖于从约束球面反卷积得到的纤维方向分布的峰值。然而，该方法会受到邻近纤维散布的偏倚，并且在角间隔低于约40-50度时失效。方法：在本工作中，我们执行一种称为通过信号拆分和迭代最小二乘估计进行纤维分割（FISSILE）的约束非线性优化，将每个体素建模为单纤维分布的混合。我们使用FISSILE在模拟数据、来自人类连接组计划的三名在体人类受试者、一只离体浣熊脑，以及同一人类受试者在两种不同信噪比（SNR）下扫描两次的数据中拟合轴对称纤维。结果：在模拟数据上，FISSILE在低至30的SNR下成功识别出角间隔为40°、体积分数为0.1的纤维，而MRtrix3的fod2fixel在相同SNR下仅限于60°间隔和0.1体积分数。在采集数据上，FISSILE在人脑和浣熊中平均分别识别出85.8%和79.4%的至少存在一条纤维交叉的白质体素，而fod2fixel分别识别出30.1%和44.5%。在具有两次采集的人类受试者中，白质纤维交叉的数量对不同噪声水平仅轻度敏感。结论：将交叉纤维现象建模为解混问题的方法提高了对单根纤维的敏感性，从而能更精确地表征解剖结构。

## Abstract
Intro: Diffusion MRI allows for in vivo probing of brain tissue architecture via micron-level diffusion aggregated at the millimeter scale, but crossing white matter fibers decrease the sensitivity of downstream tasks such as microstructural modeling and tractography. Current fixel-based methods for identifying the number, orientation, and spread of fibers rely on finding the peaks of fiber orientation distributions from constrained spherical deconvolution. However, this method is biased by the spreads of nearby fibers and fails at angular separations lower than approximately 40-50 degrees.   Methods: In this work, we perform a constrained nonlinear optimization called fiber segmentation by signal splitting and iterative least squares estimation (FISSILE), modeling each voxel as a mixture of single-fiber distributions. We use FISSILE to fit axially symmetric fibers in simulations and on three in vivo human subjects from the Human Connectome Project, an ex vivo raccoon brain, as well as the same human subject scanned twice at two different signal-to-noise ratios (SNRs).   Results: On simulated data, FISSILE successfully identifies fibers at an angular separation of 40° and a volume fraction of 0.1 at an SNR as low as 30, while MRtrix3's fod2fixel is limited to 60° separation and 0.1 volume fraction at the same SNR. On acquired data, FISSILE identified 85.8% of white matter voxels on average in human brains and 79.4% in the raccoon with at least one fiber crossing, whereas fod2fixel identified 30.1% and 44.5%. The number of white matter fiber crossings was mildly sensitive to different noise levels in the human subject with two acquisitions.   Conclusion: A method that models the crossing fiber phenomenon as an unmixing problem increases sensitivity to individual fibers for more precise characterization of anatomy.
