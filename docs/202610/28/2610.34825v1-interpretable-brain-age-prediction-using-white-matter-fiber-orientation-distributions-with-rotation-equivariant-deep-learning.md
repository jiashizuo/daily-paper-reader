## 摘要
扩散MRI（dMRI）可提供白质微结构信息，从而支持脑年龄预测。然而，许多现有预测模型使用扩散张量成像（DTI）标量指标，忽略了方向信息。深度学习越来越多地用于脑年龄预测，但传统CNN并未显式遵循方向相关特征的几何结构。我们提出了一种可解释的脑年龄预测框架，将白质纤维方向分布（WM-FODs）与SE(3)（三维特殊欧几里得群）等变CNN相结合。该网络直接作用于FOD的球谐（SH）系数，并保持其在旋转下的变换性质。在来自人类连接组计划老龄化数据集的692名36-90岁参与者中进行评估，结果表明该组合框架优于所评估的基于DTI的模型和传统CNN。对五个交叉验证模型的预测取平均，得到平均绝对误差为3.54岁，皮尔逊相关系数为0.958。我们的结果表明，性能优势依赖于将WM-FODs与等变处理相结合。预测准确率在最大SH阶数Lmax = 2时最高，并在更高阶数时下降。纤维束级遮挡（依次掩蔽每个分割纤维束）揭示了模型敏感性的年龄依赖模式，重要性峰值出现在36-44岁的额叶投射通路、45-54岁的穹窿、55-64岁的胼胝体峡部、65-74岁的小脑中脚以及75-90岁的胼胝体膝部。这些发现支持对WM-FODs进行旋转等变处理，以实现准确且具有解剖可解释模型敏感性的脑年龄预测。

## Abstract
Diffusion MRI (dMRI) provides information about white matter microstructure that can support brain age prediction. However, many existing prediction models use diffusion tensor imaging (DTI) scalar metrics that omit orientation information. Deep learning is increasingly used for brain age prediction, but conventional CNNs do not explicitly respect the geometric structure of orientation-dependent features. We present an interpretable brain age prediction framework that combines white matter fiber orientation distributions (WM-FODs) with an SE(3) (Special Euclidean group in 3 dimensions) equivariant CNN. The network operates directly on spherical harmonic (SH) coefficients of the FOD, preserving their transformation properties under rotation. Evaluation in 692 participants aged 36-90 years from the Human Connectome Project Aging dataset showed that the combined framework outperformed the evaluated DTI-based models and conventional CNNs. Averaging predictions from five cross-validation models yielded a mean absolute error of 3.54 years and Pearson's correlation of 0.958. Our results indicated that the performance advantage depended on combining WM-FODs with equivariant processing. Prediction accuracy was highest at a maximum SH order of Lmax = 2 and declined at higher orders. Tract-level occlusion, where each segmented tract was masked in turn, revealed age-dependent patterns of model sensitivity, with importance peaks in frontal projection pathways at 36-44 years, the fornix at 45-54 years, the callosal isthmus at 55-64 years, the middle cerebellar peduncle at 65-74 years, and the callosal genu at 75-90 years. These findings support rotation-equivariant processing of WM-FODs for accurate brain age prediction with anatomically interpretable model sensitivity.
