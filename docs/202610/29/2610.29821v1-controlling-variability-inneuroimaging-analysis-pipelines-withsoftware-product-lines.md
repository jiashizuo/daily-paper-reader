## 摘要
磁共振成像（MRI）是一种用于医学诊断的采集技术。它尤其用于结构 MRI 和功能 MRI（或 fMRI）图像分析。MRI 数据已知复杂且含噪，因此人们构建流程来提取并增强所获取图像中的相关信息。NARPS 研究强调了在试图回答关于脑功能的同一研究问题时，由独立团队建立的不同流程可能产生的显著变异性。用不同实现完成同一任务是软件产品线（SPL）的典型应用场景，而这一点可能未被神经影像从业者所注意。因此，基于这些不同流程提出一个 SPL，归根结底是一个典型的逆向工程问题，并伴随其常见困难。在本文中，我们主张从概念上采用 SPL 原则，作为提高神经影像研究可重复性和一致性的一条有前景的路径。

## Abstract
Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) is an acquisition technique used for medical diagnosis. It is notably used for structural and functional MRI (or fMRI) image analysis. MRI data is known to be complex and noisy, so pipelines are created to extract and enhance relevant information in retrieved images. The NARPS study highlighted the significant variability that can emerge from different pipelines set up by independent teams while trying to answer the same research question about brain functioning. Having different implementations realizing the same task is a typical playground for software product lines (SPLs), which may be out of the sight of neuroimage practitioners. Thus, proposing an SPL out of these different pipelines falls back on a typical reverse-engineering problem with its usual difficulties. In this document, we advocate for the conceptual adoption of SPL principles as a promising direction to improve reproducibility and consistency in neuroimaging research.
