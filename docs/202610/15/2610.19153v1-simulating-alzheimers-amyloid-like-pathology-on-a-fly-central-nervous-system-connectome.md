## 摘要
在阿尔茨海默病中，脑内病理改变的数量并不能完美预测功能丧失的程度。病理改变通过神经回路导致功能丧失，但这些回路无法在患者中逐神经元观察。因此，本研究模拟了成年雄性黑腹果蝇的中枢神经系统：由MaleCNS连接组连接的166,700个整合-发放神经元。该模拟器复现了一个已发表的果蝇脑模型（放电率相关性0.9997）。在假定幅度下，施加了在淀粉样蛋白-β果蝇中报告的三种损伤——锋电位阈值降低、突触丢失和神经元丢失，并在1,424只模拟果蝇中测量了回路反应。丢失的神经元数量对缺陷的指示性很差；缺陷部分取决于伴随这些神经元丢失的突触接触有多少，以及其中是否包括输入神经元或一个指令神经元。在同样去除12%神经元的情况下，当损伤落在连接稀疏、随机或高度连接的神经元上时，气味诱发的全脑活动分别为健康值的93%、47%或31%；这些果蝇分别丢失了20%、35%和48%的接触。当丢失的接触被均衡后，三组之间的差异变小；剩余差异是否反映了损伤发生的位置，尚无法确定。在48只果蝇中，仅恢复存活神经元所丢失的接触，挽回了全脑缺陷的62%；恢复被去除的神经元则挽回了39%。在丢失了传递气味信号的神经元的果蝇中，这些神经元约占神经元丢失所致缺陷的一半。一个神经元可以决定结果：所有66只丢失了受刺激侧巨大纤维（其指令逃逸）的果蝇，其巨大纤维放电率均低于100 Hz，而保留该纤维的254只果蝇中仅有1只如此。全脑活动能在比学习指数更轻的严重程度下揭示损伤，因为相对于其在各次试验间的变异，全脑活动的变化很大。

## Abstract
In Alzheimer's disease, the amount of pathology in the brain is an imperfect predictor of how much function is lost. Pathology leads to that loss through neural circuits, but these cannot be observed neuron by neuron in patients. This study therefore simulated the central nervous system of the adult male Drosophila melanogaster: 166,700 integrate-and-fire neurons wired by the MaleCNS connectome. The simulator reproduced a published fly brain model (rate correlation 0.9997). Three kinds of damage reported in amyloid-$β$ flies, a lowered spike threshold, synapse loss and neuron loss, were imposed with assumed magnitudes, and circuit responses were measured in 1,424 simulated flies. The number of neurons lost was a poor guide to the deficit, which depended partly on how many synaptic contacts went with them and whether they included input neurons or one command neuron. With the same 12% of neurons removed, odour-evoked whole-brain activity was 93%, 47% or 31% of the healthy value when damage fell on poorly connected, random or highly connected neurons; these flies lost 20%, 35% and 48% of contacts. When the contacts lost were equalised, the three groups differed less; whether the remaining difference reflects where the damage fell could not be established. In 48 flies, restoring only the contacts that surviving neurons had lost recovered 62% of the whole-brain deficit, and restoring the removed neurons 39%. In flies that had lost neurons delivering the odour, these accounted for about half the deficit due to neuron loss. One neuron could decide an outcome: all 66 flies that lost the giant fibre on the stimulated side, which commands escape, had a giant fibre rate below 100 Hz, against 1 of 254 that kept it. Whole-brain activity revealed damage at milder severity than the learning index, because it changed much relative to its variation between trials.
