## 摘要
基于表面的皮层分析在许多应用中很有用，因为它是建模大脑皮层高度折叠结构的自然方式，使得曲率和厚度等量易于计算。现有深度学习方法主要关注1×1×1 mm³ T1加权扫描，从而复现了经典流程。在此，我们提出一个神经网络，仅使用具有随机对比度和分辨率的合成扫描进行训练。因此，它几乎可应用于任何类型的磁共振成像扫描。此外，我们的模型提供预测表面的逐顶点不确定性估计，以及对fsaverage空间的球面配准。我们的模型在1×1×1 mm³ T1w扫描上与其他最先进的DL方法表现相当，并优于唯一另一种能够从低分辨率、非T1w扫描中估计皮层表面的方法。此外，预测的不确定性与低分辨率扫描的整体扫描质量相关，并在1×1×1 mm³ T1w扫描中突出显示表面重建较差的区域，表明它可用于质量控制。最后，预测的球面配准与其他DL和经典配准方法相当。这些特性与快速推理时间相结合，将提供分析现有大型临床MRI扫描数据库的能力，并有望发现疾病特异性模式，而这些在研究环境中原本可能无法实现。

## Abstract
Surface-based cortical analysis is useful in many applications as it is a natural way to model the highly folded structure of the cerebral cortex, allowing quantities such as curvature and thickness to be easily calculated. Existing deep learning (DL) methods have focused on 1x1x1 mm3 T1-weighted (T1w) scans, thus replicating classical pipelines. Here we present a neural network that is trained exclusively on synthetic scans with random contrast and resolution. Consequently, it can be applied to almost any kind of magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scan. In addition, our model provides vertex-wise uncertainty estimates of the predicted surface as well as a spherical registration to fsaverage space. Our model performs on par with other state-of-the-art DL methods on 1x1x1 mm3 T1w scans and outperforms the only other method capable of estimating cortical surfaces from low resolution, non-T1w scans. The predicted uncertainty, moreover, correlates with overall scan quality on low resolution scans and highlights areas of poor surface reconstruction on 1x1x1 mm3 T1w scans, suggesting that it can be used for quality control. Finally, the predicted spherical registrations prove comparable to other DL and classical registration methods. These qualities, when combined with the fast inference time, will provide the ability to analyze large, existing databases of clinical MRI scans, with the potential for finding disease-specific patterns that would otherwise be infeasible in a research setting.
