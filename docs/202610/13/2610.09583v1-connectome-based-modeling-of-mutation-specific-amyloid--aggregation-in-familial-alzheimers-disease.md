## 摘要
家族性淀粉样β（Aβ）变异体会改变聚集动力学，但其与脑结构连接性的相互作用仍未被完全理解。我们开发了一个突变感知的机制模型，将粗粒化的单体-寡聚体-纤维聚集-断裂系统与540节点布达佩斯参考连接组组件上的图扩散相耦合。实验测得的Aβ42成核评分将七种变异体相对于野生型的初级成核速率进行缩放。稳健性通过以下方式检验：突变评分不确定性、替代动力学映射、全局敏感性分析、种子和边权重扰动、保持度分布的随机化连接组、空间传播分析、合成ABC-SMC参数恢复、后验预测以及化学朗之万模拟。E22G表现出最早的阈值跨越和最大的累积寡聚体负荷，而在所选映射下A2V出现延迟。突变排序在所测试的网络扰动中均保持稳定，尽管区域负荷模式取决于拓扑结构。与种子区域的连接组距离与寡聚体到达较晚相关（Spearman ρ≈0.92）。在推断的参数不确定性下，平均时间排序相关性为0.990，累积负荷排序在每次后验抽样中均得以保持，而精确峰值幅度排序则较不稳定。尽管轨迹之间存在重叠，随机集合中位数仍保留了确定性排序。在该概念验证框架内，突变依赖性动力学主要影响聚集时间和累积负荷，而连接性塑造空间传播。名义背景速率、模型时间单位以及合成参数恢复将解释限制于机制性比较，而非临床校准预测。

## Abstract
Familial amyloid-$β$ (A$β$) variants alter aggregation kinetics, but their interaction with structural brain connectivity remains incompletely understood. We developed a mutation-aware mechanistic model coupling a coarse-grained monomer--oligomer--fibril aggregation--fragmentation system to graph diffusion on the 540-node Budapest Reference Connectome component. Experimental A$β_{42}$ nucleation scores scaled primary nucleation rates for seven variants relative to wild type. Robustness was examined using mutation-score uncertainty, alternative kinetic mappings, global sensitivity analysis, seed and edge-weight perturbations, degree-preserving randomized connectomes, spatial propagation analysis, synthetic ABC-SMC parameter recovery, posterior prediction, and Chemical Langevin simulations. E22G showed the earliest threshold crossing and greatest cumulative oligomer burden, whereas A2V was delayed under the selected mapping. Mutation rankings persisted across tested network perturbations, although regional burden patterns depended on topology. Connectome distance from seed regions was associated with later oligomer arrival (Spearman $ρ\approx0.92$). Under inferred parameter uncertainty, the mean timing-rank correlation was 0.990 and cumulative-burden ordering was preserved in every posterior draw, while exact peak-amplitude ordering was less stable. Stochastic ensemble medians retained the deterministic ordering despite overlap among trajectories. Within this proof-of-concept framework, mutation-dependent kinetics primarily influence aggregation timing and cumulative burden, while connectivity shapes spatial propagation. Nominal background rates, model-time units, and synthetic parameter recovery limit interpretation to mechanistic comparisons rather than clinically calibrated prediction.
