## 摘要
背景/目的：快速区分缺血性与出血性卒中亚型对急性期管理至关重要。尽管当前深度学习模型在单一数据集上可达到较高准确率，但在外部验证中仍存在“捷径学习”和域偏移问题。本研究提出一种模拟急诊分诊的分层计算机辅助诊断（CAD）系统。方法：我们引入三通道放射学聚焦预处理流程，通过非对称格式（.mat与.png）将放射学亨斯菲尔德单位（HU）窗输入卷积神经网络（CNN）和视觉Transformer（ViT）主干。该架构采用一种新颖的联合交叉注意力融合网络，并由集成正交-焦点损失进行优化。结果：我们的系统在内部卒中数据集上取得95.48%的F1分数。然而，使用Mask Anchor过滤器进行多中心外部验证（AISD、BHSD、PhysioNet）时，性能下降至58%–62%区间，暴露出由硬件噪声导致的“内部验证错觉”。关键的是，模型对缺血性卒中表现出高精确率（77%）但低敏感度（57%），证明其倾向于过度诊断出血。多种子方差分析表明，DenseNet201+ViT主干具有最低偏差（0.59）和最高稳定性，使其成为真实世界临床集成中最可靠的架构。结论：为克服诊断挑战，未来研究必须聚焦于将三维体积提取与临床数据相结合的多模态架构。

## Abstract
Background/Objectives: Rapid differentiation of ischemic and hemorrhagic stroke subtypes is vital for acute management. Although current deep learning models achieve high single-dataset accuracy, they suffer from "shortcut learning" and domain shift in external validation. This study proposes a hierarchical computer-aided diagnostic (CAD) system mimicking emergency triage. Methods: We introduce a 3-channel Radiology-Focused Preprocessing pipeline that feeds radiological Hounsfield Unit (HU) windows into Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) and Vision Transformer (ViT) backbones via asymmetric formats (.mat and .png). The architecture utilizes a novel joint cross-attention fusion network optimized by an Integrated Orthogonal-Focal Loss. Results: Our system achieved a 95.48% F1-score on the internal stroke dataset. However, multi-center external validation (AISD, BHSD, PhysioNet) using a Mask Anchor filter revealed a drop to the 58-62% range, exposing the "internal validation illusion" caused by hardware noise. Critically, models exhibited high precision (77%) but low sensitivity (57%) for ischemic stroke, proving prone to hemorrhage overdiagnosis. Multi-seed variance analysis demonstrated that the DenseNet201+ViT backbone yielded the lowest deviation (0.59) and highest stability, making it the most reliable architecture for real-world clinical integration. Conclusions: To overcome diagnostic challenges, future research must focus on multimodal architectures that combine 3D volumetric extraction with clinical data.
