## 摘要
作为两种最常见的神经退行性疾病（NDDs），阿尔茨海默病（AD）与帕金森病（PD）在症状学和病理生理学上均存在共性。然而，这两种不同疾病在多大程度上以及以何种方式重叠仍未被充分探索。在本研究中，我们提出了一种显式、可解释且具有空间自适应能力的迁移学习框架，以阐明与AD和PD相关的形态学标志物。我们将这种高维机器学习模型应用于两个最常用的开源神经影像数据集，即阿尔茨海默病神经影像学倡议（ADNI）和帕金森病进展标志物倡议（PPMI）。一方面，我们的跨疾病分类器所揭示的神经解剖学改变模式与既往神经影像研究结果高度一致，并凸显了患者的临床表现。另一方面，使用共享先验进行空间自适应训练的分类器相比各自的独立模型取得了统计学上更优的诊断性能，这进一步凸显了该跨疾病框架在建模跨疾病领域的有限规模数据集方面的有效性。

## Abstract
As two most prevalent neurodegenerative diseases (NDDs), Alzheimer's disease (AD) and Parkinson's disease (PD) shared commonalities both in symptomology and pathophysiology. However, to what extent and in what way these two distinct disorders overlap remain underexplored. In this study, we propose an explicit, interpretable and spatially adaptive transfer learning framework to elucidate the morphometric biomarkers linked to AD and PD. We applied such high-dimensional machine learning model to two most popular open-source neuroimaging datasets, i.e., Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) and Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative (PPMI). On the one hand, our cross-disorder classifier revealed neuro-anatomical alteration patterns well aligning with previous neuroimaging research findings and underscoring patients' clinical presentations. On the other hand, the spatially adaptive trained classifier using shared prior achieved statistically superior diagnostic performance compared to respective individual models, which further highlights the cross-disorder framework's efficacy on modeling limited-size datasets across disease domains.
