## 摘要
强连通分量（SCC）刻画了有向网络中的模块结构，但对单节点故障很脆弱。我们研究了强双连通分量（SBC），即在移除任意单个节点后，每一对节点仍保持相互可达的节点集合，作为一种更稳健的连通性概念。使用生成函数形式，我们推导了巨大SBC的大小，并分析了其在随机节点和链接移除下的渗流行为。我们表明，巨大SBC与巨大SCC在同一阈值出现，但由于更严格的连通性要求而增长更慢。我们还将我们的理论框架应用于真实的生物网络，包括基因调控网络和神经连接组。我们的框架为复杂有向系统中连通性、冗余性和鲁棒性之间的相互作用提供了洞察。

## Abstract
Strongly connected components (SCCs) characterize modular structure in directed networks but are fragile to single node failures. We study strongly biconnected components (SBCs), which are the set of nodes in which every node pair remains mutually reachable after the removal of any single node, as a more robust notion of connectivity. Using a generating function formalism, we derive the size of the giant SBC and analyze its percolation behavior under random node and link removal. We show that the giant SBC emerges at the same threshold as the giant SCC but grows more slowly due to stricter connectivity requirements. We also applied our theoretical framework to real-world biological networks including gene regulatory networks and neural connectome. Our framework provides insight into the interplay between connectivity, redundancy, and robustness in complex directed systems.
