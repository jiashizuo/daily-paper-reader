## 摘要
潜在轨迹分析是一种统计方法，通过根据结局变量的相似性将患者划分为同质亚组来解释异质性。在临床工作中，患者通常不遵循相同的疾病病程或治疗反应，传统分析通常对患者进行平均，掩盖了重要的亚组。本研究提出了一种新颖的潜在轨迹模型，用于混合类型的多元纵向结局，并使用期望最大化算法作为估计策略。所提出的模型能够识别个体水平、时间特定的潜在类别成员身份，以及描述潜在类别成员身份随时间变化的潜在轨迹成员身份。通过捕捉这些动态变化，我们可以突出高风险不良结局的患者，揭示改善或衰退的早期指标，并最终支持更个体化的治疗计划。我们将该方法应用于阿尔茨海默病神经影像学计划（ADNI），这是一项纵向、多中心、观察性研究，旨在验证用于阿尔茨海默病（AD）临床试验的生物标志物。

## Abstract
Latent trajectory analysis is a statistical method for explaining heterogeneity by partitioning patients into homogeneous subgroups based on similarities in outcome variables. In the context of clinical work, patients often do not follow the same course of illness or treatment response, and traditional analyses often average across patients, masking important subgroups. This work proposes a novel latent trajectory model for multivariate longitudinal outcomes with mixed types and uses the expectation-maximization algorithm as an estimation strategy. The proposed model can identify individual-level, time specific latent class memberships and a latent trajectory membership that describes how the latent class memberships change over time. By capturing these dynamic changes, we can highlight patients at higher risk of poor outcomes, reveal early indicators of improvement or decline, and ultimately support more individualized treatment planning. We present an application of our methodology to the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI), a longitudinal, multi-center, observational study to validate biomarkers for Alzheimer disease (AD) clinical trials.
